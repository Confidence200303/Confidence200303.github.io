# 01. Agile Team Charter: Platform Ready Onboarding Playbook (Zuke)

## Evidence

### 1. Project Purpose

The purpose of the Platform Ready Onboarding Playbook is to provide a structured guide that helps businesses prepare to join and use the Zuke platform. It explains the onboarding process and the digital tools businesses need to establish their online presence and support their growth.

As a Junior Frontend Developer completing Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) through CAPACITI, I developed the playbook to make onboarding information clearer, more organised and easier for businesses to follow.

### 2. Objectives

- Develop a comprehensive onboarding playbook for businesses joining Zuke.
- Provide relevant guidance for four user categories: Ideators, Hustlers, SMEs and Enterprise.
- Organise onboarding information into clear, practical sections.
- Cover key areas such as business information, business email and profile setup, social media presence, email signatures and platform registration.
- Help businesses understand how to use digital tools and AI-assisted content creation to promote their products and services.
- Improve the accessibility and consistency of onboarding guidance.

### 4. Working Agreements

- **Communication:** Used workplace communication and discussions to clarify onboarding requirements( Used TEAMS, WhatsApp, Google Meet, Zoho Emails).
- **Check-ins and feedback:** Sought guidance and feedback from my supervisor when required.
- **Review and approval:** Submitted the playbook for supervisor review and approval.
- **Confidentiality:** Protected client information, business details, login credentials and API keys. Restricted platform screenshots are not included in this portfolio.
- **Accuracy:** Ensured the content reflected the intended onboarding process and the requirements communicated by the team.

### 5. Constraints

- Client and platform information could not be shared without permission.
- Access to certain platform information and activities was restricted.
- The playbook needed to reflect Zuke's onboarding requirements and address different business categories.
- Changes to the existing platform required appropriate planning and approval.

### 6. Definition of Done (DoD)

The playbook was considered complete when the required onboarding topics were documented, the four user categories were addressed, the information was organised into clear sections, and the document was submitted to my supervisor for review and approval.

### 7. Supporting Artefacts

- The Platform Ready Onboarding Playbook, or an approved excerpt.
- A contents page showing the structure of the playbook.
- A high-level onboarding workflow created without confidential information.
- My CAPACITI WIL logbook entry documenting the activity and learning outcomes.

## Reflection

### 1. How did I decide what each type of user needed to know?

I used the onboarding requirements and guidance provided by the team to organise information for Ideators, Hustlers, SMEs and Enterprise users. I recognised that different business categories may have different needs and levels of support. This helped me structure the playbook so that the guidance would be relevant to the intended audience.

### 2. How did I organise the playbook so a new business could follow it without help?

I organised most of the playbook myself, using clear sections covering the key onboarding requirements. The aim was to present information in a logical order, from preparing business information and digital communication tools to establishing an online presence and registering on the platform. This approach helps businesses understand what they need to prepare and which steps to follow.

### 3. What feedback did I get, and what did I change?

My supervisor reviewed or approved the playbook. This gave me the opportunity to align the document with the organisation's onboarding requirements. The experience taught me the importance of following project expectations, incorporating guidance where necessary and ensuring that documentation is suitable for its intended users.

### 4. What would I do differently next time?

In future, I would confirm the requirements for each user category at the beginning of the project and create a checklist before writing. I would also seek feedback at different stages and test the instructions from a new user's perspective. This would help me identify gaps earlier and make the documentation even clearer and easier to use.
