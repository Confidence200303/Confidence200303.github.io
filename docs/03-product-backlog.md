# 03. Product Backlog: Platform Ready Onboarding Playbook (Zuke)

## Evidence

The product backlog identifies and prioritises the requirements for the Platform Ready Onboarding Playbook. The MoSCoW method was used retrospectively to classify requirements according to their importance to the onboarding process.

**MoSCoW prioritisation:**
- **Must have:** Essential requirements for the playbook to fulfil its purpose.
- **Should have:** Important requirements that improve the usefulness of the playbook.
- **Could have:** Optional enhancements that add value if time and resources allow.
- **Won't have this time:** Items excluded from the current scope.

### Why This Order?

The backlog prioritised the core information businesses need to understand and prepare for onboarding. Business information, the onboarding sequence, user-category guidance, email requirements and registration were prioritised because they support the playbook's main purpose. Additional visual aids and screenshots were treated as optional enhancements because they improve usability but are subject to time, availability and confidentiality requirements.

## Reflection

### 1. How did I decide what mattered most?

I prioritised the requirements that were essential for a business to understand the onboarding process. This included identifying the information businesses need to prepare, explaining the relevant steps and addressing the needs of Ideators, Hustlers, SMEs and Enterprise users. I treated visual enhancements as secondary because the playbook first needed to communicate accurate and useful information.

### 2. Did priorities change? Why?

The priorities were influenced by the need to keep the playbook practical, clear and aligned with Zuke's onboarding requirements. Confidentiality also affected the use of supporting screenshots because client and platform information could not be shared without permission. This reinforced the importance of prioritising useful documentation while respecting access restrictions.

### 3. What would I add or remove if I started again?

If I started again, I would define the backlog at the beginning of the project, assign clear acceptance criteria to each item and track progress as the work developed. I would also consider adding a visual workflow and a final usability checklist to help identify gaps before supervisor review. This would make progress easier to monitor and provide a clearer link between user needs, planned tasks and completed outcomes.
