### Sprint review and retrospective

**What went well:**

I was able to take the onboarding requirements and organise them into a structured playbook. Writing most of the document helped me practise analysing user needs, organising information and explaining technical processes in a way that businesses could understand. The supervisor’s review and approval also provided a checkpoint for the completed work.

**What did not go well:**

One challenge was ensuring that the guidance was relevant to different business categories while keeping the document clear and manageable. Another limitation was that not every part of the onboarding process could be demonstrated through screenshots because business and platform information needed to remain confidential.

**What I will change:**

In future, I would confirm the requirements and expected deliverables at the beginning, create a checklist for each section, and request feedback at earlier stages. I would also plan suitable visual examples or a generic workflow diagram to make the instructions easier to follow, while ensuring that confidential information is protected.

## Reflection

**1. What patterns do you see across your entries?**

A pattern across my work was the need to organise information, consider the needs of different users and review the clarity of the final document. I learned that developing a useful onboarding resource involves more than writing information; it also requires understanding the process and presenting the steps in a logical order.

**2. How did you grow between your first and latest entry?**

I developed a better understanding of how to approach a workplace task in stages, from identifying requirements to organising content and submitting it for review. I also improved my ability to communicate technical information clearly and consider the experience of someone using the document for the first time.

**3. How does this connect to your work at Zuke and your studies?**

This experience connects to my Junior Frontend Developer placement at Zuke because both onboarding documentation and frontend development require an understanding of user needs, clear information and a logical user journey. It also relates to my ICT Application Development studies, where requirements analysis, system design, usability and testing are important. The experience helped me see how technical knowledge can be applied to practical business needs.
